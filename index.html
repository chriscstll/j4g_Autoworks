ABOUT J4G AUTOWORKS

We share your passion for exploration and experiencing the great outdoors.

J4G Autoworks came about in 2020 with a flagship store located in Tanauan City, Batangas. The shop specialized in selling and installing aftermarket offroad or all terrain upgrade and wheels&tires brought about by the growing demand from the influx of new car models. The well-appointed offroad parts and accessories shop also became the perfect venue to showcase J4G Autoworks' wide range of wheels to customers and dealers alike.

In 2026, J4G Autoworks foresaw the emerging 4x4/Off-Road trend composed of pick-ups and SUVs.Brought about by severe weather conditions and unfavorable road conditions, it became a popular daily driver for most people.
